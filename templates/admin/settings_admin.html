SYSTEM ENFORCED

Strict scheduling rules enforced system-wide during schedule generation, conflict validation, room and faculty assignment, and publishing. All changes are automatically saved to the database and take effect on the next schedule generation or validation run.

WEEKEND RESTRICTION

Weekend NSTP Requirement

All courses scheduled on Sunday must be NSTP courses. No other subjects can be set for Sundays.

LABORATORY

Laboratory Room Requirement

When enabled, subjects with laboratory hours are required to use laboratory rooms for their lab sessions. When disabled, lab subjects may still be scheduled in regular lecture rooms.

Lab room validation enforced during schedule generation, auto-scheduling, and manual assignment.
FACULTY QUALIFICATION

Faculty Specialization Restriction

When disabled, subjects can only be assigned to faculty whose specialization matches the subject's curriculum program area. If disabled, specialization will be ignored.

Validated automatically during schedule generation and official scheduling.
