Meet the System

PUP Lopez Academic Scheduling System

The PUP Lopez Academic Scheduling System (PUPLC) is a web-based Decision Support System (DSS) designed for the PUP Lopez, Quezon Campus. It brings class scheduling for all academic programs into one centralized workspace, providing data-driven recommendations based on historical schedules and validating proposed schedules against scheduling constraints. The system assists the Academic Head in creating and managing feasible class schedules while keeping the final decision and control in their hands.

  • Decision Support
  • Centralized Scheduling
  • Constraint Validation
  • Data-Driven Recommendations

How It Works

From setup to an approved schedule

1

Set up the term

Administrator

Set the academic year and semester, rooms and buildings, and the teaching-load limits for each faculty type.

2

Assign loads and rooms

Academic Head

Import subject offerings and faculty records, assign teaching loads by faculty qualifications, and match each course to a lecture or laboratory room.

3

Create the schedule

Academic Head
  • Auto-generate: the system finds similar schedules from past semesters and optimizes them into a draft.
  • Manual: schedule in Room, Program or Faculty view, with ranked recommendations as you go.
4

Check for conflicts

Automatic

Every schedule is checked for duplicate faculty assignments, double-booked rooms, overloads and student schedule conflicts. Conflicts are flagged so they can be fixed before moving on.

5

Review and approve

Academic Head

The Academic Head gives the draft a final review. Once approved, it becomes the official schedule for everyone in the system.

6

Request and adjust

Faculty & Academic Head

Faculty view their schedules and send room requests, such as for make-up or remedial classes. The Academic Head approves or rejects them, and schedules can be updated when needed.

What powers steps 3 and 4

Learn from previous schedules while keeping every new assignment aligned with current scheduling requirements.

CBR

Case-Based Reasoning

Retrieves similar historical scheduling cases as a starting point for generation.

RF

Random Forest

Ranks and recommends suitable assignments during manual scheduling.

GA

Genetic Algorithm

Optimizes schedule assignments during automatic generation.

CSP

Constraint Satisfaction

Validates every schedule, whether generated, manual or modified, against current constraints.

Why We Built It

Building on how scheduling is done today

The Problem

Scheduling at PUP Lopez Campus has relied on printed forms, spreadsheets and close coordination between offices, guided by the experience of the Academic Head and program coordinators. Every semester's schedules are kept as records, and they hold years of useful decisions.

With more than 20 academic programs to schedule, that careful work takes time. The next step is to make it easier to reuse what already works.

  • 5–6 hrsaverage manual scheduling time per program
  • 20+academic programs to schedule

The Solution

The proposed system turns years of scheduling records into a useful resource for creating new schedules. Relevant past cases can be retrieved to recommend possible assignments for faculty, rooms, subjects, and time slots, while current scheduling constraints determine whether those recommendations are suitable.

By combining past scheduling experience with current requirements, the system reduces repetitive manual work and gives the Academic Head better information to evaluate scheduling options and make the final decision.
